1. Who we are

The Rushi Hair & Beauty Studio & Academy is a hair, beauty, bridal makeup and training studio located at Back of the Bus Stand, Ganesh Nagar, Near Engg. Works, Ishwarpur, Maharashtra 415409, India. We decide why and how the personal information described on this page is used.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to respond to you and manage your appointment. We collect it only when you choose to send it to us:

  • Booking request form: your name, phone number, the service you are interested in, preferred date and time, and optionally your email address and notes.
  • Contact form: your name, phone number, optional email address and your message.
  • WhatsApp, phone and in-person: anything you share with us when you message or call, such as your name, number and the details of your enquiry.
  • Technical data: like any website, the server that hosts this site may automatically record basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We do not use this to identify you personally.

We do not collect payment card details, bank details, passwords or OTPs through this website, and we will never ask you for them through the booking or contact form.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and confirm, change or cancel your appointment.
  • To discuss your requirements, for example bridal bookings or Academy course enquiries.
  • To run and improve our services and keep the business records we need.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising profiles, and we only send you promotional messages if you have agreed to receive them.

4. Other services involved

To run this website and receive your requests, some trusted services handle limited information on our behalf or when you use their features:

  • WhatsApp (Meta): when you tap a WhatsApp button, or submit a booking request, WhatsApp opens with your message ready to send. Messages you send are handled under WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • Formspree: where the online form is enabled, the details you type into the booking or contact form are delivered to us through Formspree, a third-party form service. They are used only to pass your request on to us.
  • Google Maps: the contact section shows an embedded Google map and a link to directions. When the map loads, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and may set its own cookies, under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Website hosting provider: the company that hosts this website stores the site files and server logs.

These providers may process data outside India. We share information only as needed for the purposes above, and otherwise only if the law requires us to.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not follow you across other websites. The only third-party content that may set its own cookies is the embedded Google map described above. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy first.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your booking and enquiry details only for as long as we need them to manage your appointments, answer your questions and keep reasonable business records. After that, we delete them or remove anything that identifies you. You can ask us to delete your details sooner at any time.

7. How we protect your information

  • We ask only for the minimum information needed, and no financial details.
  • This website is delivered over an encrypted HTTPS connection, so what you type is protected while it travels.
  • Form entries are checked and handled so that they cannot run code on the page.
  • Links to other sites open in a separate tab and do not get access to this page.
  • Only our own team can see your booking details, and they are used only for your appointment.
  • The devices and accounts we use to receive requests are protected with passcodes or passwords.

No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If we ever become aware of a breach that affects your personal information, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Stay safe: we will never ask for your card number, bank details, UPI PIN or an OTP through this website or by message. If someone asks for these in our name, do not share them and contact us on the number below.

8. Your rights and choices

In line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may at any time:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or out of date;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • withdraw your consent to us using your information, for example to stop promotional messages.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 11. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children and the Academy

Our salon services and this website are meant for adults. If you are under 18 and would like to book a service or enquire about an Academy course, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us on your behalf. We do not knowingly collect information from children without a parent or guardian’s consent.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add a new feature to the website. The “last updated” date at the top of the page will always show the latest version.
